The Best Movies According to Nivedhitha

My top 3 movies of all-time.

Om Shanthi Oshana

This movie is so humorous and is an OG malayalam film.

Mozhi

This movie shows that love can be expresssed in any form and is built with trust.

The characters in this movie show resilience,sarcasm,loss and a few more genuine emotions.

It showcases power of self belief and sheer determination.

To all the boys I've loved before

This movie is a heartwarming romantic comedy about a teenage girl who receives letters from her crush.
